## v1.0.0

- First public release.
- Packaged apps for Linux and Windows, and an Intel Mac build.

## v1.1.0

- Improved compatibility with Ollama.
- Added a slider that lets you adjust the chat memory.
- Added a more convenient way of switching between Ollama and LM-studio.
- Fixed a bug that would leave the app running after its tab was closed.
- Reworked settings interface.

## v1.2.0

- Added external providers: Claude, OpenAI, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, or any OpenAI-compatible service. Messages go to the provider you choose (see the README's privacy section).
- Clicking a character's picture in a chat opens a zoomable view.
- Back and Cancel return to the screen you came from, with the same character and chat.
- Green confirmation when saving or deleting a character.
- Reworked header logo and composer animations (regenerate and send).
- The server-not-running message now appears only at startup and when a message can't be sent.
- The app now stops about one second after the last tab closes.
